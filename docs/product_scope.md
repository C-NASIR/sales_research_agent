# Product scope

## What the product does

Prospecting Agent is intended to help sales and growth teams research target companies, score fit, draft outreach, review outputs, and export approved prospects from a single local workspace.

## Who it is for

The MVP is aimed at operators, founders, sales teams, and go-to-market researchers who want a structured workflow for evaluating accounts before outreach.

## What Phase 0 includes

- Repository structure for a backend, frontend, docs, data, and samples
- Minimal FastAPI service with a health check
- Minimal Next.js app with an MVP landing page
- Local setup instructions and sample company CSV

## What Phase 0 does not include

- Database models or persistence
- CSV upload or campaign creation flows
- Agent orchestration or Deep Agents integration
- Research, scoring, review, export, auth, or CRM features
